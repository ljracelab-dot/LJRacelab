Everyone thinks 'make it lighter' means 'make it thinner'. It almost never does.

I'll measure the stiffness of each version with a hanging mass and a caliper, then compute a lightweighting efficiency score. Above 1.0 means the redesign was actually smarter. Below means I just made it weaker.
