Logbook

Write it up

Fill this in and it writes the file for you. You never have to touch HTML — and you never have to remember the format, because the form is the format.

Numbers, not adjectives. "Much lighter" is worth nothing. "33% lighter, 34.2 g → 22.8 g" is worth a lot. Same effort.

01 Problem

What needed to exist, and why. An honest small reason beats an invented big one.

02 Hypothesis

What you predicted, with a number, before you started.

03 Design

Your approach — and what you considered and rejected. Rejected options are the strongest evidence of real thinking.

04 Build

Settings, problems, what surprised you.

05 Test

Method. What you changed, what you held constant, how many trials.

06 Data

A table. Units in the headers. Paste it as markdown or just list the numbers.

07 Failure

The most valuable section. What broke, and why — the mechanism, not the event. Do not clean this up.

08 Redesign & result

What you changed, and whether the hypothesis held. Numbers.

09 What I'd do next

One or two concrete steps. Shows the thinking didn't stop at the deadline.
